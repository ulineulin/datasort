What is Data?

Data is information we collect by counting or looking at things. When we count fruits, animals, or toys — that's data!

🍎🍎🍎 3 apples
🐶🐶🐶🐶 4 dogs
⭐ 1 star
🚗🚗 2 cars

🎯 Every time you count, you are collecting data!

What is a Bar Chart?

A chart is a picture of our data. It helps us see which thing has more and which has fewer — just by looking!
